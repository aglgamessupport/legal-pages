Privacy Policy — Jelly Monsters

Effective date: October 26, 2026 · Package: com.jellymonsters.game

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Jelly Monsters ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Jelly Monsters is a single-player casual game. It does not ask you to create an account with us, does not let you enter personal information such as your name, email or phone number, and does not include chat or messaging between players. We do not run our own servers. The only data we store ourselves is a small public game profile (your jelly's look), kept in our Google Firebase project and described below.

Information We Collect

Google Play Games Services. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the App uses it for:

  • Your Play Games player profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar), shown in leaderboards and achievements
  • Leaderboard scores and achievements you earn in the App
  • Cloud save — a copy of your game progress (scores, levels and stars, coins, boosters, stickers, daily rewards and quests, your jellies' looks and outfits, purchases unlocked in the game, settings) stored in your Google account so you can restore it on another device

This data is stored by Google under your Google account, not by us. Signing in to Play Games is optional — the App works without it.

Public game profile (Google Firebase). If you sign in to Google Play Games, the App keeps a small game profile in our Firebase project (Cloud Firestore), so that other players can see your jelly next to your name in leaderboards. It contains only:

  • Your Play Games player ID and a Firebase user ID created for it by Firebase Authentication
  • Your jelly's look: the jelly you picked for your profile and the in-game items it wears (for example background, hat, glasses or outfit), stored as item codes

The profile contains no name, email, phone number, location or other personal details. Your jelly's look is visible to other players of the App. Only you can change your profile. It is kept while you play and is deleted on request (see Your Choices and Data Deletion).

Advertising (Google AdMob). The App shows ads (full-screen ads between rounds and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards). AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and app instance identifiers
  • Device and OS information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (city/region level, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, clicked, watched) and diagnostic information

Ads may be shown to all users in all regions. This data is used to show, measure and improve ads, to limit how often ads appear, and to prevent fraud. Ads may be personalized based on this data. Where local law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent or lets you opt out through Google's consent form; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the App.

Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. It may collect:

  • App instance identifier, device and OS information
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (city/region level)
  • Usage events (app opens, session length, screens viewed, levels played, in-game actions, app version, crashes)

This data is processed by Google on our behalf and is not linked to your name, email or any other directly identifying information.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details. The App only receives the information needed to confirm a purchase and grant the item (product, purchase status and a purchase token).

Network time check. To verify certain time-based in-game rewards, the App may make a simple request to a Google server to read the current time. No personal data is sent with this request.

Locally stored data. The App saves your game progress and settings (scores, levels and stars, coins, boosters, stickers, daily rewards and quests, your jellies' looks and outfits, sound/vibration/language preferences) on your device using Android's local storage. It is deleted if you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Permissions

The App requests only: Internet and network state (ads, analytics, Play Games, game profile, purchases), Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) and vibration (haptic feedback). It does not access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Games Services — sign-in, leaderboards, achievements and cloud save.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, described above.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — usage analytics, described above.
  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google) — sign-in for and storage of the public game profile, described above.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the Google services listed above, for the purposes described: advertising (AdMob), analytics (Firebase), game features (Play Games and Firebase) and purchases (Play Billing). Your gamer name, score and jelly's look are shown to other players in leaderboards. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads. In regions where consent is required, you can change your ad consent from the App.
  • Play Games data: you can sign out of Play Games, and delete your Play Games profile and game data (including cloud save, achievements and scores) in the Google Play Games app or at your Google Account.
  • Public game profile: deleting your Play Games data does not remove it. To have it deleted, email us at aglgames.support@gmail.com with your Play Games gamer name; we delete it within 30 days.
  • Local data: uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes all progress and settings stored on your device.
  • Analytics data: it is retained by Google for a limited period and then deleted automatically. You can ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app instance by contacting us.

Data Security

We do not operate our own servers. The public game profile is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, where access rules let each player change only their own profile. Data handled by Google Play Games, AdMob, Firebase and Google Play Billing is transmitted over encrypted connections and secured according to Google's own infrastructure and policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The "Effective date" above will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
