Privacy Policy — Flux Loop

Effective date: August 22, 2026 · Package: com.connectorgame

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Flux Loop ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Flux Loop is a single-player offline puzzle game — it does not require an account, does not let you enter any personal information, and does not include chat, multiplayer, or social features.

Information We Collect

Automatically collected data (via Firebase Analytics). The App uses Google Firebase Analytics, which automatically collects standard app-usage data even without any custom tracking code added by us. This may include:

  • App instance identifier, device and OS information (device model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (city/region level, not precise GPS)
  • Basic usage events (app opens, session length, screens viewed, app version, crashes)

This data is processed by Google on our behalf and is not linked to your name, email, or any other directly identifying information collected by us, since we don't collect any.

Locally stored data. The App saves your game progress and settings (unlocked levels, completed levels, sound/vibration/theme preferences) directly on your device using Android's local storage (SharedPreferences). This data is never transmitted anywhere — it stays on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — usage analytics, described above.
  • Google Play Services / Play Core (In-App Review) — used only to show the standard Google Play "rate this app" prompt at appropriate moments; this is a standard Google Play API and does not send any additional data specific to the App.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. The App contains no ad SDKs. Data collected by Firebase Analytics is processed under Google's own privacy terms, linked above.

Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since the App collects no directly identifying information from any user, there is no personal data to knowingly collect from children under 13 or the applicable age of digital consent in your region.

Your Choices

You can limit ad/analytics identifiers used by Google at the OS level via your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (reset or delete your advertising identifier). Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored progress and settings from your device.

Data Security

Since the App does not collect personal information and stores gameplay data only locally on your device, there is no server-side database of user data to secure on our end. Data handled by Firebase Analytics is secured according to Google's own infrastructure and policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The "Effective date" above will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
